Five Trails You Should Hike in Harrisonburg

There are so many amazing trails in Harrisonburg that it can be a bit overwhelming choosing which one to start with or go to next. To help alleviete your choice paralysis, we have compiled a list of our personal favorite hikes.

1. Kaylor Knob

Kaylor Knob is a fairly short trail with not too much elevation gain, and a distance of 3.6 miles. At a decent pace you should be able to get to the end of the trail within 2-3 hours depending on pace, with plently of time for breaks.
For more information about Kaylor Knob, read it's trail guide here: Kaylor Knob Trail Guide

2. Narrowback Mountain

Narrowback Mountain is a nice paved trail with a distance of 10 miles. The elevation change is spread between those 10 miles making the climb up fairly easy. With a steady pace Narrowback Mountain can be finished in 1-2 hours. Be aware that you will be sharing this trail a lot of bikers, so try to stay to the right side of the trail like you would in a car. Stay aware of your surrounding and the bikes will not be an issue at all.
For more information about Narrowback Mountain, read it's trail guide here: Narrowback Mountain Trail Guide

3. Hidden Rocks

With a distance of 2.2 miles and low elevation change, Hidden Rocks is a nice trail for beginners. It only takes around 1.5-2 hours to complete the trail, and there are two streams to enjoy. Try taking a break at one of them, it's very calming to just relax to the sounds of moving water.
For more information about Hidden Rocks, read it's trail guide here: Hidden Rocks Trail Guide

4. Furnace Mountain

Furnace mountain is a bit more challenging of a hike, but its worth the extra effort for the amazing view at the summit. It's distance is 2.8 miles and it should take around 2-3. Some sections of the trail do get a bit rocky which is where most of the challenge lies, so just slow down and hike thoughtfully.
For more information about Furnace Mountain, read it's trail guide here: Furnace Mountain Trail Guide

5. High Knob Fire Tower

This hike has a distance of 2.5 miles, and should take around 1.5-2.5 hours depending on your pace. It's a bit challenging as it does have some elevation gain, but at the summit you will be rewarded with amazing views from inside of a historic fire tower!
For more information about High Knob Fire Tower, read it's trail guide here: High Knob Fire Tower Trail Guide
