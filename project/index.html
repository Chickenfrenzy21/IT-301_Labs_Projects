What is Harrisonburg Hikes?

Harrisonburg Hikes is a website for hikers to find new trails in the Harrisonburg area. This website aims to be a tool for hikers to help get people outside and exploring the beauty of nature around them. Whether you are an experienced hiker looking for a new challenge, or a novice looking where to start, we hope you find something new here.
