Hiking Tips

Are you new to hiking? If so, we reccomend you read the below tips to help you prepare for your first hike.

Tip #1: Check the weather

Before you even get on the mountain, make sure to check the weather so that you can bring appropriate clothing for the expected conditions. Trust us, you don't want to be caught out climbing on the mountain soaked to the bone because you didn't bring a raincoat. Always check the weather, and if the conditions are dangerous never feel bad for cancelling or postponing your hike. Your safety comes first.

Tip #2: Start small

If you are new to hiking, don't immediently jump to the cool challenging trails. It's important to learn how to navigate the trail and how to pace yourself properly, and it's much safer to learn these skills on a shorter hike. The mountains aren't going anywhere, so take your time and practice on those shorter trails first.

Tip #3: Pay attention to elevation

Miles aren't the only factor that determines how hard a trail is. Elevation changes specifically ascent are just as important as mileage when it comes to hiking. Don't underestimate a trail with a short mileage but high elevation gain.
